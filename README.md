# 🚀 BizInsight AI

> **AI-Powered Business Intelligence Platform for Data Analysis, Machine Learning, Forecasting, Anomaly Detection, RAG & AI-Powered Business Insights**

BizInsight AI is a full-stack intelligent Business Intelligence platform designed to transform raw business data into actionable insights.

The platform combines **React, TypeScript, Spring Boot, Python Machine Learning, XGBoost, RAG, AI Assistant, Data Analytics, Forecasting, and Anomaly Detection** into a single modern application.

# 🎯 Overview

Businesses generate large amounts of data from sales, customers, products, regions, transactions, and operations.

However, raw data does not directly provide business decisions.

BizInsight AI provides an intelligent platform that allows users to:

- Upload business datasets
- Validate and understand data
- Analyze business performance
- Generate AI-powered insights
- Forecast future revenue and sales
- Detect abnormal business behavior
- Ask questions using natural language
- Search business documents
- Use RAG-based knowledge retrieval
- Interact with an AI Business Assistant
- Make data-driven decisions

The main goal is:

```text
RAW BUSINESS DATA
        ↓
DATA PROCESSING
        ↓
DATA ANALYSIS
        ↓
MACHINE LEARNING
        ↓
AI INSIGHTS
        ↓
BUSINESS DECISIONS
